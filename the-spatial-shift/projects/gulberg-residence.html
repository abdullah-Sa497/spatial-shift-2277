The Gulberg Residence

A 1970s family home reimagined as a light-filled sanctuary for three generations.

Location
Gulberg III, Lahore
Type
Family home, renovation
Size
2 Kanal · 9,000 sq ft
Completed
2025
Scope
Turnkey design & build

(01) Overview

Project overview

The family wanted to keep the soul of the house their grandparents built, while opening it up to light, air and the rhythm of modern family life.

The brief

Three generations share this home, and it had to work for all of them: generous rooms for weekend gatherings, quiet corners for reading and prayer, and a kitchen that could handle both everyday meals and Eid feasts.

Our approach

We removed two internal walls to create a continuous living and dining space, restored the original arched windows, and layered lime plaster, Ziarat marble and hand-carved sheesham panels against a calm, contemporary backdrop. Every main room now looks onto the garden.
