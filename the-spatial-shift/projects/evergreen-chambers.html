(01) Overview

Project overview

Legal matters are stressful. These chambers were designed to feel reassuring from the moment a client walks in.

The brief

The partners wanted to move away from dark, formal interiors toward a space that felt modern, warm and quietly authoritative.

Our approach

Soft green walls, oak joinery and garden views create a calm backdrop. A residential-style client lounge replaces the traditional waiting room, and acoustic detailing keeps every conversation private.
