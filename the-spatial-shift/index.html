(04) How we work

A calm, transparent process.

Five clear stages, one dedicated team and no surprises, whether we are designing a single room or delivering a complete turnkey home.

  1. 01

    Week 1

    Discover

    We visit your space, listen closely and learn how you live, work and entertain.

  2. 02

    Weeks 2–4

    Concept

    Layouts, mood and materials come together in a concept presentation with 3D visuals.

  3. 03

    Weeks 4–8

    Design development

    Every detail is resolved: drawings, joinery, lighting, finishes and a transparent BOQ.

  4. 04

    Months 2–6

    Build & craft

    Our site team and artisans bring the design to life, with weekly progress updates.

  5. 05

    Final week

    Style & handover

    We furnish, style and hand over a space that is ready to live in, down to the last cushion.
