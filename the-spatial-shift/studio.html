(Studio) About The Spatial Shift

Design rooted in Lahore, shaped around you.

We are a multidisciplinary studio of interior designers, architects and makers, creating spaces that feel effortless to live in and are built to last for generations.

Scroll

(01) Our story

A studio built on listening.

The Spatial Shift began with a simple belief: the spaces we live and work in quietly shape how we feel every single day.

Founded in Lahore in 2014, the studio has grown into a multidisciplinary team of interior designers, architects and project managers, supported by a trusted network of craftspeople across Punjab.

We work where contemporary design meets local craft. A hand-carved Chiniot panel beside a minimalist sofa. Lime-plastered walls that keep a room cool through June. Brass that will age beautifully for decades. Our spaces are calm, not cold; considered, not precious.

Most of all, we listen. Every project starts with understanding how you live: the morning chai, the Sunday family lunches, the corner where you like to read. We design outward from there.

(02) What we believe

Four principles, every project.

01

Light first

We design around Lahore’s light, soft in winter and fierce in summer, so every room feels right at every hour.

02

Honest materials

Stone, timber, lime and brass: natural materials that age gracefully and feel good to the touch.

03

Rooted in place

Contemporary spaces that carry a quiet memory of local craft, climate and heritage.

04

Made for living

Beautiful is not enough. Every room has to work for real family life, every single day.

(03) Craft & materials

Made by hand, made to last.

From Chiniot’s master woodcarvers to Multan’s blue-pottery artisans, we partner with craftspeople whose skills have been passed down for generations, and design pieces that let their work shine.
