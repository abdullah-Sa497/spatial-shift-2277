(FAQ) Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you’re looking for? Ask us directly.

How much does interior design cost in Lahore?

Every project is different. Fees depend on the size of your space, the scope of work and the level of finish. After a site visit we share a clear, itemised proposal, with our design fee and a transparent bill of quantities for execution, so there are no surprises.

How long does a typical project take?

A single room can be designed in three to four weeks. A full home usually takes eight to twelve weeks to design and four to eight months to build, depending on scope and approvals.

Do you work outside Lahore, or with overseas clients?

Yes. We regularly work in Islamabad and Karachi, and many of our clients are overseas Pakistanis building back home. We run video consultations, send detailed WhatsApp updates and keep a dedicated project manager on your site.

Can I use my own contractor?

Absolutely. With our full interior design service we provide detailed drawings and specifications, and we visit site regularly to make sure everything is built as designed.

Will I see my space before it is built?

Yes. We produce photorealistic 3D visuals and physical material boards, so you can see and touch your finishes before anything is ordered.

Do you design furniture?

We do. Much of the furniture and joinery in our projects is designed in-house and made to measure by our network of craftspeople, from solid sheesham tables to upholstered headboards.
